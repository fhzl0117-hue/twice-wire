SEOUL — AUGUST 2026

Ten years in,
nine still together.

TWICE just marked ten years since their 2015 debut with #BEST 2015-2025, a 50-track, four-disc career-spanning compilation released August 26. It lands weeks after Jeongyeon (August 10) and Chaeyoung (August 13) each ended their exclusive contracts with JYP Entertainment to manage solo work independently — Jeongyeon through newly-signed VARO Entertainment, Chaeyoung through her own "Lil Fantasy" banner — while both explicitly confirmed they're staying in TWICE, with group activities continuing under JYP as before. The group's This Is For World Tour wrapped in July after 81 shows, its biggest run yet. This page is a working build of how TWICE Wire tracks all of it for English-speaking ONCE — translated, sourced, and converted to whatever timezone you're reading from.

SEOUL — AUG 10–13

Jeongyeon and Chaeyoung both exit JYP's exclusive contract — both stay in TWICE

Jeongyeon signed with newly-formed VARO Entertainment for solo work on August 10; Chaeyoung followed on August 13 with her own independent "Lil Fantasy" banner. Both explicitly said the change affects solo management only — group activities continue under JYP as before. Jihyo and Tzuyu are separately reported to be weighing similar moves, unconfirmed as of this writing.

Summarized from Forbes and K-Crush by TWICE Wire staff.

TOKYO — AUG 26

#BEST 2015-2025 lands: a 50-track, four-disc decade in full

The Japanese anniversary compilation spans two Korean-repertoire discs (2015–2019 and 2020–2025) and two matching Japanese-language discs, running from "Like OOH-AHH" through "This Is For" and "Me+You." No new material is included — this is a career victory lap, not a comeback.

Summarized from Billboard Philippines by TWICE Wire staff.

Read the original source ↗

SEOUL — JUL 12

This Is For World Tour wraps after 81 shows, the group's biggest run yet

The tour launched July 2025 in Incheon, added a North America–Europe "In the Round" leg through spring 2026 including three sold-out nights at Tokyo's National Stadium — the first non-Japanese act to headline there solo — and closed with three encore shows at Seoul's KSPO Dome.

Desk 02 · Review

Revisiting the catalogue, track by track.

Text only, always — no reposted photos or video frames on this desk.

3.9 / 5EDITOR'S STAMP
"Nine solo showcases dressed up as a group album — generous to the members, a little exhausting as a single sitting, and worth it for at least three of the nine detours."

Ten: The Story Goes On (2025): TWICE's 10th-anniversary album, and the closest thing to a full-cast introduction the group has released. Lead single "Me+You" is a warm, '90s-leaning R&B-pop track about the group's relationship with fans; the other nine tracks are individual member solo songs, most previewed earlier on the This Is For World Tour.

Critics split roughly down the middle: Clash gave it 8/10, praising how far the members' individual styles had evolved; AllMusic was cooler at 3/5, calling it a fairly standard hip-hop-inflected pop outing. Both are fair reads of the same record — it plays better as nine spotlight moments than as nine songs meant to cohere.
